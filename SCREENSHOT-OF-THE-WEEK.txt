Screenshot of the week
======================

To update the featured shot on navapron.com:

1. Overwrite this file (same name):
     screenshot-of-the-week.png

2. Optional — edit the caption under
     <!-- Screenshot of the week -->
   in index.html

3. Cache bust — in index.html change:
     screenshot-of-the-week.png?v=1
   to ?v=2 (then 3, …) after you upload, so browsers
   pick up the new image.

4. Deploy / push the site as you usually do.
