Privacy Policy

NavApron · Last updated: 30 August 2026
Applies to the NavApron desktop app and the website at navapron.com.

Short version: NavApron is designed to keep your flight data on your PC. We do not sell personal data. Optional anonymous community totals can be shared if you leave that setting on. Third-party map, weather, and network services receive only what those features need to work.

1. Who we are

NavApron is an independent freeware companion for Microsoft Flight Simulator, developed by Rob Air (“we”, “us”). It is not affiliated with Microsoft, Asobo, or Navigraph/SimBrief.

Contact for privacy questions: NavApron Discord or buymeacoffee.com/robair.

2. What NavApron stores on your device

Most information stays local on your computer (or tablet when using LAN sync), including:

  • Airport chart caches and recent airports
  • Airline profile, logbook, and career stats you create
  • Saved flight plans and share-card images (e.g. under Documents\NavApron)
  • Preferences (SimBrief username/ID if you enter one, map settings, window size, LAN toggle)
  • An anonymous install ID (random UUID) used only for optional community stats

We do not require an account to use NavApron. Local files remain under your control; uninstalling or deleting the app’s data folders removes them from that device.

3. Information we may receive (optional / feature-based)

3.1 Community flight totals (opt-out available)

If community sharing is enabled in Settings, when you complete a logged flight NavApron may send a small anonymous event to servers we operate (via navapron.com), such as:

  • Anonymous install ID (not your name, callsign, or airline)
  • Departure / destination ICAO
  • Block time, airborne time, distance
  • App version and a flight event id

This is used only for aggregate community counters shown in the app. You can turn sharing off in NavApron Settings at any time. No email address is collected for this feature.

3.2 App updates

Portable (zip) installs may check navapron.com for a newer version (version.json). That request typically includes normal web server logs (IP address, time, user-agent). Microsoft Store installs update through the Store instead.

3.3 Website visits

When you visit navapron.com, our hosting provider may process standard server logs (IP address, browser type, pages requested, timestamps) for security and reliability. We do not run advertising trackers on the NavApron marketing site for the purpose of selling your data.

4. Third-party services the app talks to

Depending on which features you use, NavApron may contact independent services. Those providers process data under their own privacy policies:

  • OpenStreetMap / Overpass — airport surface charts
  • OurAirports — airport and navaid reference data (first download / refresh)
  • Aviation weather / METAR sources — live airport weather when shown
  • CARTO / OpenStreetMap tiles — map basemap imagery
  • RainViewer — optional weather radar tiles
  • VATSIM / IVAO — optional live network traffic overlays
  • SimBrief — if you generate or import an OFP (your SimBrief credentials/session stay with SimBrief; NavApron stores the username/ID you type locally and imports the OFP you request)
  • Microsoft Flight Simulator / SimConnect — live aircraft data stays on your PC between the sim and NavApron

We do not control those services. Use only the features you are comfortable with.

5. Local network (PC ↔ phone / iPad)

If you enable LAN / phone access, NavApron can share planning session data on your home Wi‑Fi. That traffic stays on your local network unless you expose your PC intentionally. Do not enable LAN access on public or untrusted networks.

6. Children

NavApron is a flight-sim tool aimed at a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for marketing. If you believe a child has provided personal data to us online, contact us and we will delete it where applicable.

7. Data retention

  • On your device: until you delete the app data or uninstall.
  • Community stats: kept as aggregate/anonymous events for community totals; not used to identify you by name.
  • Server logs: retained only as long as needed for security and operations (typically a short rolling period set by hosting).

8. Your choices

  • Turn off community stats sharing in NavApron Settings
  • Leave SimBrief username blank if you do not use SimBrief import
  • Disable VATSIM / IVAO / weather overlays you do not want
  • Disable LAN access
  • Delete local data folders (install data folder and/or Documents\NavApron)

9. Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect services we operate. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. Keep your PC updated and download NavApron only from official links (navapron.com / Microsoft Store when published).

10. International users

NavApron is offered worldwide. If you are in the EEA/UK, you may have rights under GDPR/UK GDPR to access, correct, or delete personal data we hold about you, and to object to certain processing. Because most processing is local on your device, many requests are fulfilled by you deleting local files. For anything we store on our servers (e.g. anonymous stats infrastructure or web logs), contact us via Discord.

11. Changes

We may update this policy when the app or website changes. The “Last updated” date at the top will change. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.

12. Microsoft Store

If you install NavApron from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft’s own terms and privacy statement also apply to Store download, licensing, and Store update delivery.
